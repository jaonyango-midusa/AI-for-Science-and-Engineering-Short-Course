# Week 12 | Ethics & Responsible AI

## Lesson Outline

In this lesson, we are going to discuss;
- Why responsible AI?
- Bias and Fairness
- Privacy, consent, and data governance
- Transparency, Explainability & Accountability
- Reliability, Safety & Trust
- Responsible Generative AI
- AI in Scientific Research
- Wider Societal & Environmental Implications
- Responsible AI in Practice

## Expected Learning Outcomes
By the end of the lesson, participants should be able to:
- Explain the principles of ethical and responsible AI.
- Identify potential sources and consequences of bias in AI systems.
- Discuss fairness, inclusion, and representation in AI datasets and models.
- Explain key considerations relating to privacy, consent, data ownership, and governance.
- Discuss the importance of transparency, explainability, accountability, and human oversight.
- Evaluate reliability, safety, and potential harms associated with deploying AI systems.
- Identify responsible-use issues associated with generative AI.
- Apply principles of research integrity and responsible AI to AI-assisted scientific work.
- Assess the broader societal and environmental implications of AI.
- Critically evaluate an AI application and propose measures for its responsible development and use.
