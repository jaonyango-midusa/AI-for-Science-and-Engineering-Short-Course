# Week 13 | Course Project Showcase

## Apply to Present Your Project

Participants who have developed a project during the course are invited to apply for an opportunity to present their work during the Course Project Showcase.

A limited number of projects will be selected for presentation. Selection will focus on the development of the project through the course, the clarity of the AI/ML workflow, and the potential value of sharing the experience with the wider class.

Your project does not need to be a finished product or use a sophisticated machine learning model. We are interested in seeing how you have taken an idea and developed it through the different stages of the course.

## What to Submit in Your Application

Please provide:

1. **Project title**
2. **Problem addressed** – Briefly describe the problem and why it is important.
3. **AI/ML approach** – Briefly describe the approach you used.
4. **What you have built** – Tell us what you currently have working (e.g., notebook, model, application or prototype).
5. **Key result or learning** – What is the most interesting result, finding, or lesson from your project?
6. **GitHub repository link** – Provide a link to your course project repository.
7. **Why would you like to present your project?**

## If Selected

Selected participants will be invited to present their project during the Week 13 Course Project Showcase.

The presentation should cover:

- The problem and why it matters
- The data used
- The AI/ML approach
- What you built
- Results and interpretation
- A demonstration of the project
- Responsible AI considerations
- Lessons learned and next steps

### Presentation Format

- 10 minutes – Project story, approach, and results
- 5 minutes – Demonstration
- 5 minutes – Questions and discussion

**Total: 20 minutes per project**

The showcase is an opportunity to share your learning and what you have built during the course. It is not an examination, and projects do not need to be fully developed or deployment-ready.
