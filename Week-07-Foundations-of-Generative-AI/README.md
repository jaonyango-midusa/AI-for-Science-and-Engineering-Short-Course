# Week 7 | Foundations of Generative AI

## Lesson Outline
In this lesson, we will talk about;
- Introduction to Generative AI
- Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Representing Language for Machine Learning - Tokenization; Embeddings
- Transformers
- Foundation Models and Large Language Models
- Pretrained Models and Hugging Face
- Prompting and Working with LLMs
- Generative AI for Science and Engineering
- Limitations and Responsible Use

## Expected Learning Outcomes
By the end of the lesson, participants should be able to:
- Explain the basic principles of generative AI and distinguish it from other ML approaches.
- Explain the role of NLP and identify common NLP tasks.
- Explain how tokenization and embeddings enable machines to work with language.
- Describe the role of attention and transformers in modern language models.
- Explain the concepts of foundation models and LLMs and how they generate outputs.
- Find and explore pretrained models using Hugging Face.
- Apply basic prompting techniques when interacting with LLMs.
- Identify applications of NLP and generative AI in science and engineering.
- Discuss limitations and responsible use of generative AI.
